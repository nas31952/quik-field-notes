# Quik Field Notes — Mobile Field GIS & Audio Notes

**Powered by Nasrullah Khan**

---

## 📌 Overview
**Quik Field Notes** is a cross-platform mobile GIS companion and field notebook designed for high-accuracy ground reconnaissance, terrain mapping, geotagged photo capture, and fast-and-furious multi-accent voice dictation.

Built to function seamlessly in remote desert and rugged field areas without cellular reception, Quik Field Notes supports offline vector mapping, local shapefile overlays, real-time heading orientation, and one-tap multi-format export.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Full-Screen Field GIS Canvas**: Clean, intuitive interface with full offline satellite, topo, and OpenStreetMap caching.
- **Dynamic Compass & Heading Navigation**:
  - Small, unobtrusive North Arrow widget.
  - Smooth compass bearing with one-tap toggle between **North-Up** and **Heading-Up** modes.
  - Point-and-go navigation to any map coordinate or feature with live distance and steering indicators.
- **Local Map Layer Overlays**:
  - Load ESRI Shapefiles (`.zip`), GPX tracks/waypoints (`.gpx`), KML (`.kml`), and GeoJSON (`.geojson`).
  - Toggle individual overlay visibility and adjust transparency sliders in real time.
- **Interactive Digitizing**:
  - Draw lines and polygons directly on the map canvas using touch or stylus.
  - Attach ground condition feedback, slope details, or vehicle accessibility notes to drawn features.
- **Discreet Map Symbols**:
  - Clean, micro-sized vector markers for waypoints, tracks, and photos to prevent screen clutter.
  - Tap any feature on the canvas to open a detailed **Feature Info Card**.
- **Multi-Project Management**:
  - Partition field data cleanly by project name, with seamless "No Project" fallback.
  - Auto-persists all waypoints, tracks, photos, and layers until explicitly cleared.
- **Customizable Waypoint Categories**:
  - Add, edit, or remove categories (Hazard, Obstacle, Access Trail, Sabkha, Dune, Water Point, Camp).
- **Fast & Furious Multi-Accent Speech Recognition**:
  - Instant on-device neural transcription supporting Arab and South Asian English accents, native Arabic, and Urdu.
- **Multi-Format Data Export**:
  - 📦 **Field Survey Package (`.scoutpkg`)**: Integrated container with all waypoints, tracks, notes, and photos.
  - 📄 **Field Notes CSV (`field_notes.csv`)**: Tabular spreadsheet ready for Excel or GIS attribute joins.
  - 🗺️ **Standard GPX (`field_data.gpx`)**: Waypoints and tracklogs for GPS receivers, BaseCamp, and GIS.
  - 📐 **GeoJSON (`features.geojson`)**: Vector layers ready for immediate import into QGIS or ArcGIS Pro.
  - 📷 **Photos Archive (`photos.zip`)**: Geotagged field photos with observation comments.

## 📲 Installation

### Android
Download and install the **Universal Android APK** (`Quik_Field_Notes_Universal.apk`) directly from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/nas31952/quik-field-notes/releases). Compatible with Samsung Galaxy Note 8 (Android 7.1+) through modern Android 15.

### iPhone & iPad (Safari PWA — Zero Install)
1. Open Safari on your iPhone and visit: **[https://nas31952.github.io/quik-field-notes/](https://nas31952.github.io/quik-field-notes/)**
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. The Quik Field Notes icon will appear on your home screen and run full-screen offline with high-precision GNSS and neural speech dictation.
